What we build

Welding, fabrication and repairs for homes, farms and businesses across Palmerston North and the Manawatū. Here's a closer look at the work we do most.

Service 01

Gates & Fencing

Whether it's a sliding gate across a long driveway or a simple side gate, every one is measured on site and built to suit the opening, the ground and how you'll use it.

  • Sliding and swing driveway gates
  • Pedestrian and side gates
  • Security and pool-style fencing
  • Steel frames for timber infill
  • Gate repairs and re-hangs
See gates photos

Service 02

Handrails & Access

A good handrail makes steps safer for everyone. We make and install rails that are strong, comfortable to grip and finished to match the house or building.

  • Step and entrance handrails
  • Deck and stair rails
  • Galvanised or painted finishes
  • Homes, schools and commercial sites
  • Balustrade panels
See handrails photos

Service 03

Trailers & Floats

From a brand-new tandem build to bringing a tired horse float back to life, trailers are a big part of what we do. Repairs are done properly so they're ready for the road.

  • New box, car and flat-deck trailers
  • Chassis and drawbar repairs
  • Axle, hub and spring replacement
  • Mudguards and guards
  • Horse float rebuilds and fit-outs
See trailers photos

Service 04

Truck & Vehicle

Custom gear that fits your vehicle and the way you work, rather than an off-the-shelf part that nearly fits.

  • Aluminium toolboxes
  • Headboards and racks
  • Roll cages
  • Brackets, mounts and modifications
See truck photos

Service 05

Farm & Stockyards

Farm gear takes a beating. We build and repair yard equipment that's made to handle stock, weather and years of hard use.

  • Race and drafting gates
  • Head bails and crush gear
  • Troughs and feeders
  • Stockyard repairs and upgrades
See farm photos

Service 06

Fabrication & Repair

If it's metal and it's broken, worn out or doesn't exist yet, chances are we can sort it. One-off jobs are welcome.

  • Excavator and loader bucket rebuilds
  • Forklift and machinery attachments
  • Canopies and steel frames
  • Sheet-metal folding
  • General welding repairs
See fabrication photos

How it works

From idea to install
