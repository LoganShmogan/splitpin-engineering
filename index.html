02 / About

One tradesman.
Start to finish.

Splitpin Engineering is a sole-trader welding and fabrication business. No call centre, no subcontracting. The person who quotes your job is the person who builds it.

That means straight answers, honest pricing and work that's done properly the first time. Whether it's a single handrail or a full trailer build, every job gets the same attention to detail.

  • Talk direct to the welder, not a middleman.
  • Built to suit, measured and made for your site.
  • Workshop or onsite, we come to you where it makes sense.
  • Domestic, farm & commercial jobs, big or small.

How it works

From idea to install

  1. Get in touch

    Call, text or send the form with a few details and photos if you have them.

  2. Measure & quote

    We'll talk it through, measure up and give you a clear, no-surprises quote.

  3. Fabricate

    Cut, fit and welded in the workshop, or onsite for repairs and installs.

  4. Deliver & install

    Finished, fitted and checked. Job done properly, site left tidy.
