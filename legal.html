1. About us

This website is operated by Splitpin Engineering, a sole-trader welding and fabrication business operating in New Zealand ("we", "us", "our"). References to "you" mean the person visiting this website or engaging our services.

NZBN: to be confirmed. GST registration details are shown on our quotes and invoices where applicable.

2. Privacy policy

We respect your privacy and handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 2020 and its Information Privacy Principles.

What we collect

  • Details you give us when you call, text, email or use the quote form, such as your name, phone number, email address, site address and job details.
  • Photos or drawings you send us of the work you need done.
  • Records of quotes, invoices and payments relating to work we carry out for you.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry, prepare quotes and carry out the work.
  • To invoice you and keep business and tax records as required by law.
  • To contact you about your job, including follow-up or warranty matters.

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't use it for unrelated marketing.

Sharing

We only share your information where needed to do the job or meet legal obligations, for example with our accountant, Inland Revenue, a supplier delivering materials to your site, or where required by law.

Storage & security

We take reasonable steps to keep your information safe and only keep it for as long as we need it for the purposes above or as required by law (for example, financial records are generally kept for seven years).

This website

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we don't run analytics that identify you. The quote form doesn't store anything on our server. It opens your own email app so you can send the message to us directly. Our hosting provider may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and performance, and fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. See our cookie policy for more detail.

Photos of our work

We may photograph completed work for our portfolio. We won't publish photos that identify you, your address or your vehicle registration without your consent. If you'd like a photo of your job removed from this site, just let us know and we'll take it down.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct it. Contact us using the details below. If you're not happy with how we've handled your information, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.

3. Terms of trade

These terms apply to all quotes and work carried out by Splitpin Engineering unless we agree otherwise in writing.

Quotes & estimates

  • Quotes are valid for 30 days unless stated otherwise, and are based on the information and measurements available at the time.
  • An estimate is our best guess at the likely cost and may change. A quote is a fixed price for the work described.
  • Changes you request, or site conditions we couldn't reasonably have known about, may be charged as variations. We'll talk to you before doing extra work.
  • Material prices can move quickly. If steel or other material costs rise significantly before your order is confirmed, we may need to re-quote.

Deposits & payment

  • A deposit may be required before materials are ordered, particularly for larger or custom jobs.
  • Unless otherwise agreed, payment is due within 7 days of the invoice date, or on completion for smaller jobs.
  • We may charge reasonable debt-collection costs on overdue accounts.

Ownership of goods

Goods we supply remain our property until they have been paid for in full. Where the Personal Property Securities Act 1999 applies, you agree we may register a security interest over goods supplied until payment is received.

Your responsibilities

  • Give us safe and reasonable access to the site and let us know about any hazards, underground services or boundary issues.
  • Make sure any building consent or other approvals required for the work are obtained, unless we've agreed in writing to arrange them.
  • Collect completed goods within a reasonable time. We may charge storage on items left more than 30 days after completion.

Workmanship & consumer law

We stand behind our work. Nothing in these terms limits your rights under the Consumer Guarantees Act 1993, the Fair Trading Act 1986 or the Contract and Commercial Law Act 2017. If something isn't right, contact us and we'll work with you to fix it.

Our workmanship doesn't cover damage caused by misuse, overloading, accidents, lack of maintenance, normal wear and tear, or modifications made by others. Customer-supplied parts or materials are used at the customer's risk.

Business customers: where you acquire our goods or services for business purposes, the parties agree that the Consumer Guarantees Act 1993 does not apply, and that sections 9, 12A, 13 and 14(1) of the Fair Trading Act 1986 do not apply to the extent permitted by law.

Liability

To the extent permitted by law, we're not liable for indirect or consequential loss (such as loss of profit or downtime), and our total liability for any claim is limited to the price paid for the work concerned.

4. Cookie policy

Cookies are small text files a website can save in your browser, often used to remember settings or track visitors across sites.

Cookies we use

None. This website does not set any cookies. We don't use analytics, advertising, social media pixels or any other tracking tools, which is why you won't see a cookie banner here.

Third-party services

  • Google Fonts: the fonts on this site are loaded from Google's servers, so your browser sends Google a standard request that includes your IP address. Google states that the Fonts service does not set cookies. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Hosting: our hosting provider (Vercel) processes standard technical information, such as IP address and browser type, to deliver the site securely. It does not use this site to set advertising or tracking cookies.
  • Phone and email links: tapping a phone number, email address or the quote form opens your own phone or email app. Anything you send is handled by that app and your email provider.

Managing cookies

You can view, block or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings. Because this site doesn't rely on cookies, blocking them won't affect how it works.

Changes

If we ever add analytics or other tools that use cookies, we'll update this policy first and ask for your consent where required.
